# Independent layout review: superseded converter snapshot

Reviewed2026-11-08; recorded2026-11-09. Scope was read-only native KiCad10 geometry inspection while the router ran. The owner subsequently rejected the TPS63020 architecture; this is historical evidence, **not approval of a current simplified board**. Board SHA256 `94feb330bf1b38ab1e55156aedabe47445dee6bfeaf437ac2697c0da2f0d2fac`,327 tracks/vias including74 vias. Raw pad/via findings: `../evidence/independent_layout_geometry.json`. No CAD or generator was changed by this reviewer.

1. **Unintended holes in bottom Ra-02 solder lands:** GND vias at(136.8,104.4), (136.8,106.4), (120.8,104.4) overlap U2 pads1,2,16. The candidate script used `pad.GetLayer()` for its same-net drill exclusion; the flipped pads returned F.Cu although their solder lands are on B.Cu. Check each pad's actual `IsOnLayer(F_Cu/B_Cu)` on both sides, including same-net pads. Only explicitly intended U1.57/U5.15 thermal via-in-pad geometries are exceptions. Copper DRC alone need not reject same-net solder-wicking holes.
2. **Thermal vias:** nine S3 and three TPS holes intentionally lie in their exposed lands; no other opposite-side pad overlap was found in that snapshot. EP paste gaps, tenting/process and final all-via hole checks remain necessary. Near-pad holes within0.25 mm of same-net copper are separately listed in the raw report; these are geometry observations, not all electrical violations or an assertion that a generic hole-to-copper rule applies within one net.
3. **Old converter feedback placement:** U5.3 to R32.1 route was7.492 mm and divider approximately5 mm from logic GND pin2, with a separate remote ground via. [TI layout section10.1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps63020.pdf) recommends local divider/control ground and short wide power loops. REG_L1/L2 routes were each3.498 mm long at0.22 mm, VIN-to-first-cap2.998 mm and VOUT-to-first-cap1.608 mm; these required local placement/copper review. **Do not spend further effort optimizing this rejected power circuit.**
4. **RF width is unresolved by simple equations:** [JLC04161H-3313](https://jlcpcb.com/impedance) provides0.0994 mm outer dielectric,Er4.1,0.035 mm outer copper. JLC mask values are0.01524 mm above trace,0.03048 mm above substrate andEr3.8; CAD's generic0.01 mm mask is not this impedance model. Simple IPC-style microstrip approximation yields49.82 Ohm at0.15 mm versus44.48 Ohm at0.18 mm. Independently evaluating [KiCad10 grounded-coplanar equations](https://gitlab.com/kicad/code/kicad/-/raw/10.0/pcb_calculator/transline/coplanar.cpp), with its own elliptic-integral convention, gives58.35/52.08 Ohm respectively with0.15 mm side gap, without mask. Results are in `../evidence/rf_geometry_crosscheck.json`. Their disagreement means narrowing to0.15 mm is not source-supported closure. The0.18 mm/0.15 mm-gap proposal remains a starting geometry pending a coated-coplanar supplier/field-solver check. Explicitly control the ground gap and continuous L2 return, then tune the assembled RF networks; the antenna corner feed segment cannot share a continuous-plane impedance claim because the antenna clearance deliberately removes that plane.
5. **USB:** J1 A6/B6 share D+, A7/B7 share D-. D1 USBLC6 pairs1/6 and3/4 feed R38/R39 and S3 GPIO20/19 respectively, consistent with the intended schematic topology. USB had not yet been routed in the examined snapshot, so no length/pair/transition claim is made. [Espressif USB layout guidance](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-hardware-design-guidelines/en/latest/esp32s3/pcb-layout-design.html#usb) requires parallel matched pairs,90 Ohm +/-10%, continuous reference and nearby ground returns at unavoidable transitions. Review the complete connector-to-ESD-to-series-resistor-to-chip route after routing; short full-speed routing alone does not close these checks.

The above findings were communicated to the root implementation agent before its subsequent work. Re-run relevant geometry against the final simplified source hash; this snapshot must not be cited as final-board acceptance.
