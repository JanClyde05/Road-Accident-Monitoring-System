## Parts to settle before assembly

- Buy or identify the exact ESP32-S3FH4R2, GCT USB4105-GF-A, C&K PCM12SMTR and PTS810SJM250SMTRLFS, PUI SMI-1027-TW-3V-R, Abracon ABM8-40.000MHZ-10-1-U-T, TPS63020DSJR, TPS22919DCKR, TLV803EA30DBZR and WS2812B-V6. Generic switches, a similar USB socket, an unknown 40 MHz crystal and unspecified DC5V WS2812B stock do not establish a compatible substitute.
- U4/Q3 require the selected Slkor DW01A and FuxinSemi FS8205A pinout. Confirm the actual six-lead parts; historical eight-lead FS8205 instructions do not apply. U3 requires a verified LTC4054ES5-4.2, not identification by the LTH7 marking alone.
- The power inductor is Coilcraft XFL4020-152ME, 1.5 uH. The regulator input/output capacitors require sufficient capacitance at their operating DC bias; TI's characterized examples are recorded in the electrical review. RF inductors/capacitors must have suitable GHz impedance/Q, tolerance and package. A matching printed value alone is insufficient for the RF path or the >=500 mA RF-supply inductor L1.
- The owner has 500 mAh and 1000 mAh nominal 3.7 V LiPo choices. Use one compatible 4.2 V-charge cell at a time. Confirm its exact model, protection arrangement, charge/discharge ratings, size and permitted temperatures. Verify J2 pin1=B_PLUS and pin2=B_MINUS with a meter against the actual harness before insertion; housing fit and wire colour are not polarity evidence.
- Obtain a 433 MHz antenna and suitable Ra-02 U.FL cable if not already present. The onboard Johanson antenna is for 2.4 GHz BLE/Wi-Fi. Provide battery insulation, connector strain relief and clearance from both antennas before enclosure testing.

## Heatbed process and assembly order

The owner confirmed the heatbed process. Historical `Antigravity Build/RAMS - PCB/JLCPCB_ORDER_PACKAGE/PARTS_LIST_FOR_ORDERING.md`, lines 162-165, names **Mechanic IX5 Ultra** and **Relife RL-403 Sn63Pb37**, with an illustrative 205-210 C step. The parts inventory does not verify those tools or paste, and the old temperature recipe has not been qualified for this new BOM. Read the actual paste label, alloy, particle size, expiry/storage and supplier profile. A heatbed display temperature is not the component-body temperature; profile a sample board with a thermocouple.

1. Confirm the final board, stencil and BOM revision agree. Check the stencil apertures for the 0.4 mm-pitch S3 and both exposed pads. Review ordinary thermal-via tenting and solder-wicking with the fabricator/stencil process; filled/capped vias have not been assumed. Keep R30 **CHG_LINK unpopulated**.
2. Inspect bare copper/holes, then dry-fit USB, slide, buttons, battery socket, buzzer and Ra-02. Check accessible actuator/socket positions, board/enclosure clearance and bottom iron access. Match part pin1/polarity to the actual top/bottom assembly drawing. Do not rotate a bottom module from a top-view drawing by intuition.
3. Print and inspect top paste, place top parts, and reflow with the bottom module absent so the board rests properly. Use the paste's process window within every fitted component's limits. The [PTS810 profile](https://www.ckswitches.com/media/1476/pts810.pdf), page2, shows a 235 C typical peak and at most two cycles; it is not permission to apply a universal 260 C profile. The [PCM switch](https://www.ckswitches.com/media/1424/pcm.pdf) specifies a 255 C solder-temperature maximum. Consult the exact crystal, connector, MCU, LED and buzzer limits as well.
4. Cool undisturbed. Inspect QFN perimeter joints, USB pin bridges, regulator, crystal and 0402 parts under magnification. Confirm the S3 EP and regulator EP have actually wetted; visible perimeter solder does not prove the hidden joints. Investigate an abnormal first article before repeating the process on the remaining chips.
5. Clean only with a process allowed by the fitted parts. The [PUI buzzer datasheet](https://api.puiaudio.com/filename/SMI-1027-TW-3V-R.pdf), Rev D revision table dated 2025-01-24, explicitly disallows washing and declares MSL2A. Keep liquids/flux out of its acoustic opening and observe moisture handling. Its protective tab does not establish permission to wash the assembly.
6. With battery and all power disconnected, support the top components in a holder and hand-solder the bottom Ra-02 castellations. Avoid loading the USB shell, buzzer or S3 against the bench. Inspect every joint, particularly all GND/VCC pads; do not reflow the completed board against the heatbed with the module underneath.

## Electrical checks before applying power

Record board serial, source/package hashes, fitted-part substitutions and clear photographs. Use a current-limited bench supply, DMM, microscope and oscilloscope. Use a floating cell simulator or an appropriately isolated supply for battery tests: a grounded supply or scope clip on B_MINUS can bypass the protection MOSFETs. Scope ground goes to protected **GND/TP1**, and differential/isolated measurement is required across the cell/protection boundary.

| Check | Acceptance |
|---|---|
| R30 | Absent. Charger input is intentionally disconnected pending cell/harness approval. |
| Main rails | No solder bridge/low-resistance short on VBUS, SYS_IN, +3V45, VDD_SPI or battery input; distinguish capacitor charging and semiconductor paths from a hard short. |
| Cell protection | B_MINUS is not directly shorted to GND by copper, solder, harness or test instruments. Q3 drain pads2/5 are joined; gates follow the exact-maker pinout. |
| Load sharing | AO3401A gate1=VBUS, source2=SYS_IN, drain3=B_PLUS; SS14 SMA cathode=SYS_IN. Verify diode polarity before using a cell. |
| U5 control | VINA pin1 is REG_VINA with local 100 nF, not externally shorted to VIN/SYS_IN. PS/SYNC13 is held high for forced PWM. The slide switches EN, not the full supply load. |
| MCU and memory | Power reaches all required domains; VDD_SPI is locally bypassed and internally supplied, not shorted to +3V45. BOOT, GPIO45 and GPIO46 straps have their specified states. |
| Test access | TP1 GND; TP2 +3V45; TP3 B_PLUS; TP4 UART_TX; TP5 UART_RX; TP6 MCU_EN; TP7 BOOT; TP8 SYS_IN. External UART must use compatible logic and must not drive an unpowered board. |

## Staged powered bring-up

1. **USB power, battery absent:** keep R30 absent and hold RESET low. Feed 5 V through a current-limited USB breakout/supply. Start with a low diagnostic current limit, then raise it only after rail and short checks; a limit set below startup demand can cause repeated resets and is not evidence of a circuit defect. Confirm expected switch-off state, then turn on and inspect SYS_IN, REG_VINA, +3V45 and regulator waveforms before releasing RESET. Stop on excess current, unstable regulation, unexpected voltage or heating.
2. **Rail acceptance:** nominal is 3.450 V, not 3.3 V. Project operating target is **3.32-3.58 V**, including ripple/transients; the ESP32 supply must never exceed 3.6 V and V6 LED required-operation supply must stay at least 3.3 V. Measure at the MCU and Ra-02, not only at the regulator. The static budget and +/-50 mV allowance in the review are calculations, not measured performance. Check idle and combined BLE, LoRa TX, RGB and buzzer loads. Forced PWM must remain enabled.
3. **Reset and battery sense:** TLV803EA30 has nominal 3.08 V falling threshold and 200 ms release delay. Confirm MCU_EN stays low through startup/brownout and rises after a stable rail. TPS22919 ON shares MCU_EN; its QOD connection is through **R40=100 Ohm** to OUT. Check ADC sense disabled when off/reset and approximately half the cell voltage when enabled. Capture supply, EN and GPIO2 during switch-off, USB insertion/removal and controlled fault collapse. Acceptance is GPIO2 <= its powered domain +0.3 V throughout. The 10k/10k/10nF network has nominal 50 us time constant; unknown effective rail capacitance and load-switch maximum timing prevent treating a simple shutdown estimate as proof.
4. **Boot and firmware:** release RESET and inspect UART boot output. To enter download mode, hold BOOT low, press/release RESET, then release BOOT. Flash the existing `firmware/wearable_withApp` with its approved build settings for 4 MB flash and 2 MB quad PSRAM. Test native USB enumeration/programming with the USB-C plug in both orientations. Do not substitute an S3 module pinout. No firmware file was changed by this hardware task.
5. **No eFuse programming on the default rail:** Espressif specifies VDD3P3_CPU <=3.3 V while burning eFuses. The default 3.45 V assembly is therefore not an eFuse-programming setup. Ordinary firmware flashing does not require changing eFuses. Any future eFuse procedure needs a separately reviewed, measured lower-voltage supply configuration.
6. **Battery operation using a simulator first:** check normal 4.2-to-3.0 V input operation, current and hot-plug transitions with controlled limits before fitting an actual LiPo. Verify that USB powers the system without forcing uncontrolled current through the battery path, that the off-state current is acceptable, and that no unintended rail backfeeds appear. Qualify the exact DW01A/FS8205A protection thresholds/recovery using a cell simulator; do not deliberately overcharge, overdischarge or short a real pouch cell for this test. The firmware's 3.0 V percentage endpoint is not a shutdown feature.
7. **Charging, only after cell/harness verification:** power down, then fit R30 if the selected cell permits the programmed charge conditions. R3=10k gives nominal 100 mA, specified 93-107 mA under LTC4054 conditions, before resistor error. Verify actual charge current, cell voltage, termination and temperature through a complete cycle using instrumentation suited to cell charging. Nominal C/10 termination is about 10 mA. Capacity labels alone do not establish a charge-current rating. Keep the load-share and charger returns on protected GND.
8. **Functions:** attach the 433 MHz antenna before transmitting. Test LoRa packets with a known receiver at 433 MHz, SF9, 125 kHz bandwidth, coding4/5, 17 dBm and sync0x34; verify SPI and DIO0/reset behavior. Test BLE pairing and phone GPS/IMU data, RGB channel order, DC buzzer, boot/cancel and alert timing. The buzzer's 30 mA bound is at rated3 V; measure its actual draw at3.45 V. Firmware does not explicitly set a BLE transmit-power limit, so a0.45 A combined-load assumption is not a guaranteed maximum.
9. **ADC and RF commissioning:** compare reported battery voltage to a calibrated DMM at several cell voltages. The equal divider preserves ratio2.0, but the existing rawADC/4095*3.3 calculation is uncalibrated; document and separately implement any required firmware calibration. Verify crystal startup/frequency and tune both RF matching sections using suitable equipment. Repeat range/current checks with the actual battery, antenna cable, enclosure and body placement. Keep metal and battery away from the B100 corner keepout.

## Evidence required to call the board working

Save measured rail/current/thermal results, reset and ADC transient captures, charge/protection results, USB orientation tests, firmware/build identity, radio functional/range results and battery calibration. Include test conditions, equipment and fitted deviations. Passing CAD checks, a successful flash, an LED blink or one received packet alone does not close the remaining tests. At this writing no physical assembly or electrical test has been performed.
