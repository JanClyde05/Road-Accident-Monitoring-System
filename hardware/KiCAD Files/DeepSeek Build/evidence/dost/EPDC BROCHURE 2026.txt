Center (EPDC) is a DOST Advanced Science and
Technology Institute (DOST-ASTI)'s strategic initia tive to
support and accelerate innovation in the electronic s
industry. Backed by seasoned professionals and equi pped
with state-of-the-art facilities, EPDC transforms i deas into
reality by offering end-to-end support for electron ics and
emerging technology development—from research and
design to manufacturing and testing.
 
By bridging the gap between concept and
commercialization, EPDC empowers startups, SMEs,
academic institutions, and industry partners to bri ng their
electronic innovations to life. Whether launching a  new
product, enhancing an existing one, or validating d esigns
for market readiness, EPDC delivers the technical

OUR SERVICES
The Electronics Product Development Center (EPDC)
offers a wide range of quality services that suppor t the
development of electronic products in the country.  
The Research, Engineering, and Development (RED)
Unit transforms innovative ideas into practical sol utions
by offering comprehensive support for Research and
Development (R&D) initiatives. With core expertise in
Hardware and Mechanical Design, the unit provides t he
foundational services essential for electronics
development. The RED Unit also offers Electronics
Equipment Servicing, specializing in the repair and
upkeep of custom and specialized equipment.  
From design to production, the Manufacturing Unit
provides end-to-end solutions—offering precise PCB
Fabrication and Assembly, along with custom 3D-
Printed enclosures—crafted to meet your product’s
technical and functional requirements.  
Whether in building, refining, or validating existi ng
electronics technology, the Test Engineering Unit
houses state-of-the-art resources for its product
testing services which include EMC Compliant Testin g
based on various standards and Product Safety Testi ng
for electrical, thermal, mechanical, and structural
reliability, and Parametric Testing.
