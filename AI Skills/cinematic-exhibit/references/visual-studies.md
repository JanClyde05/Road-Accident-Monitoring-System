# Visual studies

Use each study for a specific decision or comparison. Open its source and asset licenses before incorporating upstream files. A demo can guide lighting or interaction without shipping its code or art.

- **Studio Glass** shows a designer’s workspace with adjustable lighting, movable blinds, a turning monitor and refractive controls. Use it to compare whether an input produces a coherent response across the room and glass. [ThreeUI Studio Glass](https://threeui.com/three-js/studio-glass)
- **Light a Three.js Workspace** builds a desk, window and blinds around a single lighting direction. Borrow the physical relationship between occluder, lit air and receiving surface when lighting a room or foliage around a shaft. [Workspace lighting skill](https://github.com/MengTo/Skills/blob/main/agent-skills/3d/light-a-threejs-workspace/SKILL.md)
- **Sylva Field Guide Layout** is an editorial layout reference. Its insets, text columns, captions and understated rules support botanical imagery. Use it to lay out a habitat exhibit or a guide to specimens. Tree geometry and planting density are separate scene decisions. [Sylva layout skill](https://github.com/MengTo/Skills/blob/main/agent-skills/web-design/sylva-field-guide-layout/SKILL.md)
- **Scroll Mesh Survey** describes a narrow reversible scan over the visible subject. Use it for an inspectable crown, component or landscape feature when the user controls progress. [Mesh survey skill](https://github.com/MengTo/Skills/blob/main/agent-skills/web-design/scroll-mesh-survey/SKILL.md)
- **Point Cloud Scan** and **Lens Raindrops** cover mesh-registered points and camera-space refraction. Keep their coordinate systems distinct when a visitor pans. [Point-cloud technique](https://github.com/MengTo/Skills/blob/main/agent-skills/game-development/build-point-cloud-scan/SKILL.md) · [Lens technique](https://github.com/MengTo/Skills/blob/main/agent-skills/game-development/build-lens-raindrops/SKILL.md)
- **Threejs-Punk Drive** and the **Three.js Conference alley** are visual references for moving-camera composition, practical lights and wet streets. The conference implementation can be inspected for its WebGPU/TSL effects. Select techniques compatible with the target renderer and venue. [Drive](https://www.threejspunk.com/) · [Conference project](https://github.com/ektogamat/threejs-conference)
- **RAEA** is a native rendering and authoring option for producing local media. It is useful when a fixed shot merits offline production. Verify the current platform support and license before choosing it. The exhibit can still run in an ordinary browser with local encoded media. [RAEA project](https://github.com/shinyobjectz/raea)
