# Illustrated road sequences

The four sequences are original educational animations. Their choreography, location, vehicle speeds and on-screen timing are illustrative. The images accompanying the no-WebGL version are still frames from the same animations.

The sedan and SUV use geometry from Kenney’s Car Kit 3.1. The scene samples the source palette at each vertex and applies exhibit-specific paint, window shading, lights and road reflections. The kit is licensed under CC0. [Creator’s distribution](https://opengameart.org/content/car-kit) · [Included license](kenney-license.txt)

The motorcycle, articulated figures, phone, wearable representation and terrain were modeled for this exhibit. The chest placement illustrates carrying the unit. The Hardware chapter contains the project’s source-bound PCB reconstructions.

During each incident, a brief highlight travels across sampled triangles of the illustrated road user or vehicle. RAMS uses the phone’s motion sensors. The surface highlight depicts the moment of contact for the audience.
