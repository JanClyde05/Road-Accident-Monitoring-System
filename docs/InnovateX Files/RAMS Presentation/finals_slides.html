Who calls when the rider cannot?

Research objective. Identify a candidate event on the phone and send it by radio to a station in range.

System contribution

What travels in 26 bytes?

RAMS links a decision made on the rider’s phone to a local radio receiver. The road-to-station LoRa hop needs a receiver in range and can operate without cellular service.

PhoneBLEWearableLoRaReceiverUSBStation

LoRa alert payload

13 B  HeaderType, device token, send-time counter
4 B  Latitude
4 B  Longitude
1 B  Event
4 B  Peak g

Registration data and cancellations each travel in a separate message.

RAMS shared firmware protocol and receiver implementation. The 26-byte payload size excludes radio overhead.

Impact and next trial

Can a useful report reach the desk in time?

On the road

Freeze the settings, then count false alarms and missed events on held-out, labeled trips.

At the receiving station

Measure packet delivery at known distances and time each event through operator acknowledgement.

In continued use

Measure hours per charge. Check which stations can keep a receiver powered and staffed.
