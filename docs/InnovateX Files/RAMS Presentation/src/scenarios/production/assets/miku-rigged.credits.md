Hatsune Miku appears as the fictional wearer in the pairing, rider, pedestrian and fall illustrations. The model is **Hatsune Miku, Vocaloid, T-Pose 3D Model** by **Leon Okafor**, released under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**. The supplied T-pose was adapted for this exhibit with a lighter textured mesh, a skeleton, skin weights, twin-tail movement, an idle pose and a walk cycle. The rider's helmet and visor, phone, wearable, pairing path and scene-specific performances were authored for RAMS.
